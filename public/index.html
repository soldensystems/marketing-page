      How responsibility transfers

      Solden earns write access. It does not assume it.

      Your prior closes are ground truth. Every capability earns its place by reproducing them before it touches the present. Four of the five stages write nothing to your ERP.

      1. Writes: none

        Connect

        Read-only, least-privilege. Every scope shown to you at grant time.

      2. Writes: none

        Map the work

        Your checklist, thresholds and approvers, encoded once and versioned.

      3. Writes: none

        Replay history

        A close you have already done, run in isolation and set beside what you reported.

      4. Writes: none

        Shadow mode

        Solden closes alongside your team. Your reviewer-time baseline is set.

      5. Writes: journals

        Live

        Only on your signed activation. Every post passes through review first.

      Your ERP stays the system of record, and it stays yours. Every stage in detail.

      The vision

      Beneath the CFO and the controller: one department, one function at a time.

      The CFO and controller stay, holding policy, judgement and fiduciary authority. Solden takes on the department beneath them in a fixed order, each function built on the trusted output of the one before. A function is added only when the one before it is repeatable.

      Accounting and the close first. Controllership, FP&A, tax and strategic finance follow, each with the same reviewer model. The department, function by function.

      The buying test

      Do not take our word for it. Replay a close you have already done.

      Your prior close is ground truth. Before Solden touches a live period, it reproduces one you have already reported, in isolation, and you set the two side by side.

      Claim

      Solden can take responsibility for your month-end close.

      Test

      Fix the entity, the period, the scope and the deliverables. Solden runs that close on its own operating ledger, a private working copy of the period's data held by Solden, never written to your ERP, and sets the result beside what you reported.

      Passes when

      Coverage, accuracy, exceptions and pack completeness match yours, or every difference is explained with evidence. An unexplained difference is a defect, and we treat it as one.

      This is how you prove it for yourself. The test in full, including what replay does not prove.
