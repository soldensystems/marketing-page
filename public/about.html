About

The finance department, done properly.

Solden is a small team with accounting, fintech, enterprise sales, design and engineering backgrounds, building the finance department, one function at a time. Reviewers at Solden are qualified, experienced accountants and controllers, named to your account.

Why Solden

As the work grew, so did the department. It no longer has to.

For decades, finance has scaled the only way it could. A new entity, a new market or a jump in volume meant another hire: a bookkeeper, then accountants, then a controller, then an FP&A analyst once the board wanted forecasts, then a CFO. When the team could not keep up, the company bought another tool or paid an outside firm for the overflow.

Each addition solved this month's problem and added to next month's. More people meant more review. More software meant more systems to reconcile, and more people to run them. More firms meant more handoffs to chase. The cost of finance rose with every step of growth, and the same few people still carried the books, the forecast, the audit and the board pack.

AI breaks that link. The recurring work of finance can now be done by software and agents, checked by people and proven line by line, so more work no longer has to mean more people, more tools and more firms.

That is the department Solden is built to be: the whole finance function, from the books and the close to FP&A and the board pack, with your CFO and controller making the decisions. When the work grows, Solden takes it on, and your team does not have to grow to carry it.

Most companies will stop building a finance department. They will run on one.

The finance department becomes infrastructure. Always running, always proven.

Like any infrastructure, it starts from the foundation. Solden takes on the department beneath your CFO and controller in a fixed order, accounting and the close first. A function is added only when the one before it is repeatable.
