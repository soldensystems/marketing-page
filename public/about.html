02 The story so far

What discovery taught us, why the close, what we are building now.

What we heard

More than thirty conversations with controllers and finance leaders across the UK, continental Europe and Africa. Most run NetSuite. Eight in ten told us the same thing in different words: they are tired of managing more systems and hiring more people to run them.

Why the close comes first

It is recurring, urgent and falsifiable. Your prior closes are ground truth, so every capability we build has to reproduce them before it touches the present. Controllership, FP&A and compliance each consume the trusted output of the close.

What we are building now

The month-end close, end to end: reconciliations, accruals, the evidence behind every figure and the review that releases it. Read-only first, on the ERP and systems you already run.

03 The order

The finance department becomes infrastructure. Always running, always proven.

The CFO and controller keep policy, judgement and fiduciary authority. Solden takes on the department beneath them in a fixed order, accounting and the close first. The order is a dependency, not a roadmap: a function is added only when the one before it is repeatable.

Controllership

Controls and approvals as a running record, audit coordination, entity operations, finance systems administration.

Reviewer: the controller

FP&A

Rolling cash, budget against actuals, forecast maintenance with every assumption versioned, board reporting.

Reviewer: the CFO

Tax and compliance

Filings prepared with complete support from attested closes, controls evidenced continuously.

Reviewer: controller or CFO

Strategic finance

Commitments, capital and the decision pack: what the numbers mean and what needs a decision.

Reviewer: the CFO

Filings, money movement, payroll approval and external representations stay with you in every function.
