About

The finance department, done properly.

Solden is a small team with accounting, fintech, enterprise sales, design and engineering backgrounds, building the month-end close first. Reviewers at Solden are qualified, experienced accountants and controllers, named to your account.

Why Solden

Finance has been staffed for decades. It has never been handed over.

Growing companies have always built finance one hire at a time: a bookkeeper, then accountants, then a controller, then a CFO, with an outside firm for whatever the team could not cover. Each hire took a piece of the work. None of them took the result. That stayed with the company, and every month it gathered in one place: the close.

Software arrived the same way, one step at a time: a tool for reconciliations, a checklist for the close, a template for accruals. The steps got faster. The month did not. On day four the controller is still chasing a bank statement for one reconciliation, an invoice for one accrual and everyone for the review, before the month can close.

What no company could buy was someone else to answer for the result. AI changes that. The recurring work of the close can now be done, evidenced and checked without a hire for every step, so the responsibility can finally move.

So Solden is built as a department. Software and agents reconcile, accrue and assemble. A qualified accountant at Solden reviews every workpaper before it reaches you. Your controller sees only the calls that need judgement, each with its evidence, and attests to a close that is already finished. And because one department prepares the whole close, every figure in the statements traces back through the workpaper that produced it to the document it came from.

Most companies will stop building a finance department. They will run on one.

The finance department becomes infrastructure. Always running, always proven.

Like any infrastructure, it starts from the foundation. Solden takes on the department beneath your CFO and controller in a fixed order, accounting and the close first. A function is added only when the one before it is repeatable.
