The department, function by function.

A finance department is something you assign work to. Solden takes it on one function at a time, in a fixed order, and every function runs on the same reviewer model, the same evidence standard and the same audit trail.

01 Accounting and the close

Fifteen workstreams of the month-end close. Solden does the work. You decide where judgement stays human.

Every workstream your controller signs today, with the one decision that stays yours marked on each.

Reconciliations

Bank reconciliations

Match by amount, date and reference. Propose near-misses. Age unmatched items.

You decide Timing or break

Intercompany

Match reciprocal balances per entity pair. Flag out-of-balance pairs.

You decide Which side is right

Balance sheet

Prove every in-scope account: expected against actual, prior items rolled forward.

You decide Stale items

Subledgers

Accounts receivable

Age receivables, apply cash, compute the bad-debt provision per policy.

You decide Provisions, write-offs

Accounts payable

Age payables, accrue goods received not invoiced, flag duplicates.

You decide Estimates without a PO

Fixed assets

Run depreciation, capitalise above threshold, reconcile the register to the GL.

You decide Capitalise or expense

Payroll

Reconcile the payroll run to GL postings by cost centre. Verify withholdings.

You decide One-off payments

Journals and schedules

Accruals and prepaids

Run amortisation schedules, post recurring accruals from templates, tie each to its source.

You decide New accruals

FX revaluation

Revalue open foreign-currency balances at period-end rates. Explain the movement.

You decide Rate disputes

Tax provisions

Compute VAT and sales-tax positions, prepare the income-tax provision per policy.

You decide Positions, filing sign-off

Revenue review

Tie recognised revenue to billings, contracts and delivery evidence. Flag cut-off.

You decide Recognition timing

Reporting and sign-off

Flux and variance

Compare every line to prior period and budget. Attach likely drivers.

You decide Commentary

Eliminations

Generate elimination entries from reconciled balances. Verify they net to zero.

You decide Disputed items

Financial statements

Assemble P&L, balance sheet and cash flow. Verify the statements articulate.

You decide Disclosures

Attest and lock

Verify completeness, assemble the pack, enforce the checklist.

You decide The attestation itself
02 The unit of delivery

Solden delivers packs, not figures.

You do not receive a number, a report or a dashboard. You receive the finished close and everything needed to trust it. Sign-off is refused while evidence is missing or an exception is open. Once locked, the pack is immutable and exports as one bundle for your auditors.

Work product

The financial statements, articulated and tied to every workstream.

Completeness checklist

What done meant for this period, each item checked.

Evidence index

Every figure mapped to its lineage, navigable from any number.

Exception log

Every exception with proposal, resolution, decider and time.

Attestation trail

Who reviewed and decided what, with the policy version each approval was judged under.

Lineage manifest

Producer, timestamp, source system and version for every record.

03 The order

Accounting and the close first. Each function after it, built on the one before.

The order is a dependency, not a roadmap. A function is added only when the one before it is repeatable.

Controllership

Controls and approvals as a running record, audit coordination, entity operations, finance systems administration.

Reviewer: the controller

FP&A

Rolling cash, budget against actuals, forecast maintenance with every assumption versioned, board reporting.

Reviewer: the CFO

Tax and compliance

Filings prepared with complete support from attested closes, controls evidenced continuously.

Reviewer: controller or CFO

Strategic finance

Commitments, capital and the decision pack: what the numbers mean and what needs a decision.

Reviewer: the CFO

Filings, money movement, payroll approval and external representations stay with you in every function.

04 Principles

What we will do, and what we will never be.

We will

  • Do the work. You review it. You never manage agents, prompts or workflows.
  • Attach evidence to every number. A figure without its trail is a draft.
  • Escalate only true judgement calls, as a case with a proposal and the evidence.
  • Price monthly by entities and volume, never by hours or seats.
  • Start read-only, and keep your ERP as the system of record.

We will never be

  • An ERP replacement.
  • An outsourced accounting firm billing hours.
  • A product you operate. If you are managing workflows, the design has failed.
