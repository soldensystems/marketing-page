Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains what information Solden Systems Ltd. collects, how we use it and the choices you have. It applies to the Solden website and services.

1. Who we are

Solden Systems Ltd. ("Solden", "we", "us") is building the AI finance department for growing companies: software that runs recurring accounting and finance operations across a customer's existing systems and escalates only the decisions that require human judgement. Solden processes financial and operational data only for responsibilities a customer has authorised. This policy covers personal data we process as a controller for our website and our contacts, and describes our role as a processor when we handle customer data on a customer's behalf.

2. Information we collect

When you contact us through this website we collect your name, work email, company and the message you send. We use this only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation.

We use privacy-preserving, cookieless analytics that count page views and button clicks in aggregate. We do not use advertising trackers.

When a customer connects Solden to its systems, we process the data needed to perform the financial responsibility the customer has authorised. That data is handled on the customer's behalf and remains the customer's.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your message and communicate with you about Solden.
  • To provide, maintain and improve the services.
  • To secure the website and services and prevent abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Legal bases

Where applicable law requires it, we process personal data on the bases of performing our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and improving the services, your consent, and compliance with legal obligations.

5. Sharing

We share personal data only with service providers who help us operate the website and services and who are bound to protect it, when required by law, or in connection with a corporate transaction. Service providers act on our instructions and under appropriate safeguards. Contact-form messages are delivered through an email provider and may be stored in a database we control.

6. Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as needed to provide the services and for legitimate business or legal purposes. Customer-data retention, export, deletion, legal hold and backup propagation are governed by the applicable service configuration and agreement.

7. Security

We use access controls, protected transport, audit logging and governed boundaries for responsibility, evidence, authority and actions. Security measures vary by service and are described in the applicable agreement. See the controls register.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. International transfers

We may process personal data in countries other than where you are located. Where we do, we use appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will take reasonable steps to notify you and will update the date above.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact compliance@soldenai.com.

Solden runs finance for growing companies.
