02 What Solden can touch, and when it can write

Five stages. Four of them write nothing.

Your past closes are the benchmark. Solden reproduces them before it works on a live period.

  1. Writes: none

    Connect

    Read-only, least-privilege, every scope shown at grant time. In your ERP, a dedicated integration role with only the permissions you approve.

  2. Writes: none

    Map the work

    Your checklist, thresholds, templates and approvers, encoded once and versioned with actor and time.

  3. Writes: none

    Replay history

    A close you have already done, run in isolation on Solden's operating ledger and set beside what you reported.

  4. Writes: none

    Shadow mode

    Solden closes the current period alongside your team, still read-only, and records how much of your time its close needs.

  5. Writes: journals

    Live

    Only after you sign the write activation. Every post passes through review first and is read back from the ERP.

The buying test

One past close, run again.

Agree the entity, the period, the scope and the deliverables. Solden runs that close on its operating ledger, never your books, and sets its result beside what you reported. You judge it: the test passes when coverage, accuracy and exceptions match what you reported, or every difference is explained with evidence.

What replay does not prove

A messy live period.

An ERP outage mid-close, or a chart of accounts changed on day two. Shadow mode tests Solden against those on a live period, and a written fallback procedure covers a close that stops halfway.

03 What you receive

A close pack, not a dashboard.

You receive the finished close and everything needed to trust it. Attestation is refused while evidence is missing or an exception is open. Once locked, the pack cannot be changed, and it exports as one bundle for your auditors.

05 Controls

The controls register your security review will ask for.

Every control is designed and written down before any connection is made. The last one is yours to keep.

Read-only until you activate writes

A separate write activation you sign, logged with actor and time.

Review gates every action

Nothing posts to your ERP or locks without passing through it.

Nothing overwritten

Policies, mappings, journals and source records are versioned. Nothing is edited in place.

Dirty data quarantined

Source data that fails mapping becomes an exception, never silently coerced.

Degrades openly

Throttled APIs, partial data and failed extractions are visible, never silent.

Credentials in a vault

Never in application databases. Encryption in transit and at rest.

Each customer's data kept apart

One customer's data can never be reached from another customer's account.

SSO and MFA

Google Workspace or SAML. MFA enforced for every role.

No training on your data

Your ledger, documents and decisions never train a model, ours or a vendor's.

Who at Solden can see it

The reviewers named to your account. Every view is logged and visible to you.

Security review

We complete your security questionnaire and share the controls register before you connect anything.

Statutory responsibility

Solden is not an auditor, a bank or a statutory signatory. That stays with you.

06 What happens when it is wrong

Every task ends checked and complete, or blocked with a name and a date.

Nothing is left half-done. A blocker says what is wrong, who must act and by when.

A break

Unmatched items, missing support, threshold breaches and duplicates surface as exceptions with a proposal. Never a silent failure.

A challenge

Your controller can challenge any step in any evidence trail. It routes back as an exception with their note attached.

An adjustment

Adjustments create new versions. The original, the change, the reason and the actor are all preserved.

A failed run

The close resumes from the last completed workstream, and the pack records what was run again and why.

An input outstanding on your side

The close cannot be verified while your inputs are outstanding. The pack records whose input each delay waited on.

An error caused by Solden

Re-performed and re-reviewed, a corrected pack version issued, and the cause recorded in the exception log.
